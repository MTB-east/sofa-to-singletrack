MTB EAST GAMES

Games

Free mini-games from MTB East CIC — built for young riders, and a couple that anyone can play. No accounts, no cost, no catches.

AGES 5–10 · UNTIMED

Trail Sign Match

A relaxed memory game — flip cards, find pairs, and pick up a real fact about MTB East and mountain biking each time you match one. No clock, no pressure, no losing.

  • Flip two cards at a time to find every pair
  • Three difficulties, from 6 pairs up to a sharper-eyes 8
  • Every match reveals a fact — kit, trail skills, race know-how
Play →
AGES 5–10 · ARCADE

Singletrack Sprint

An endless trail runner — jump roots, duck branches, dodge puddles, and collect skill badges named after real trail-riding skills as you go.

  • One-tap controls: tap top to jump, bottom to duck
  • The trail gets faster and trickier the further you get
  • Badges are named after real skills — ask a coach to show you one
Play →
ALL AGES · QUIZ

Trailhead Trivia

200+ questions on technique, kit, trail etiquette, grading and MTB East's own race calendar, across Mixed, Daily Challenge and Picture rounds.

  • Badges and streaks for regular players
  • A shared leaderboard — see how you rank
  • A new Daily Challenge every day
